Affiliate Program Terms & Conditions

By CK 9 articles

What is the CK Capital Affiliate Program?

The affiliate program allows approved creators and traders to earn commissions by referring customers to CK Capital.

How do I become an affiliate?

Apply through the affiliate application process. Each application is reviewed individually.

When are affiliate payouts processed?

Affiliate payouts are processed according to our payout schedule. A minimum payout $250 is required before a payout can be requested or processed.

What type of content is not permitted?

Affiliates must not publish, promote, or distribute any content that is misleading, deceptive, false, offensive, defamatory, unlawful or likely to damage the reputation, brand or interests of the company.

What commission do affiliates earn?

Approved affiliates can earn up to 25% commission on qualified referrals, subject to their affiliate tier, performance and program terms.

Can my affiliate account be reviewed or terminated?

Yes. All affiliate accounts are subject to ongoing review. CK Capital reserves the right to suspend or terminate any affiliate account or partnership at its sole discretion if it violates program rules, engages in prohibited activity or is no longer aligned with the company objectives.

How can I update my affiliate information?

If you need to update your affiliate information, please contact our support team and provide the required details for verification. Once your identity has been confirmed, our team will review and process the requested changes as soon as possible.

What happens if I become inactive?

Inactive affiliate accounts may be reviewed, suspended or removed from the program.

Can CK Capital change the affiliate program terms?

Yes. CK Capital reserves the right to update affiliate commissions, requirements, benefits and program terms at any time.
